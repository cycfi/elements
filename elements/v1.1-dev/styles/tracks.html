Tracks

Overview

A track is the groove a slider’s thumb runs along (see Sliders). There are two: a plain track, and a fill track, which fills part of itself to show a value. With a fill track, one look serves a slider, a range slider, a progress bar, a busy bar and a VU meter.

A fill track in a slider fills from the start up to the thumb: from the left, or from the bottom of a vertical track. In a range slider, it fills between the two thumbs. As the foreground of a status bar (see Status Bars), it fills the part the bar gives it: up to the value of a progress bar, or the moving segment of a busy bar.

A fill track may be filled in bands of color along its length instead of one color: green, then yellow, then red, for a VU meter. In a status bar, the bands are placed along the whole bar, so each band shows only once the value reaches it. A fill track may also be filled in a smooth gradient through color stops, placed the same way.

A track has round ends, or square ends: its shape is a template argument, track_shape::round or track_shape::square. The stock status bars are square. A fill track may glow with glow(): the fill is drawn with a soft halo in its own color, each band in the band’s color. The glow takes room at both sides of the track, as much as the track is thick. Each track is at least 64 long, and as long as it is given. Its colors are set by chaining, basic_fill_track<5>().fill_color(c), and a track holds a color only when one is set: by default, it holds none.

Example

A slider whose track fills up to its thumb, in teal on a gray track:

#include <elements/element/style/track.hpp>
#include <elements/element/style/slider.hpp>   // For basic_thumb
#include <elements/element/slider.hpp>         // For slider
...
using namespace cycfi::elements;
...
auto level = slider(
   basic_thumb<20>(),
   basic_fill_track<5>()                        (1)
      .track_color(rgba(60, 60, 64, 255))
      .fill_color(colors::light_sea_green),
   0.8
);
1 A fill track, 5 thick, filled up to the thumb.

Radial Fill Track

A radial fill track is a ring around a dial’s knob: a track along the dial’s travel, filled from its start up to the dial’s value. Like radial_marks (see Sliders, Dials and Thumbwheels), it wraps the knob, and takes the dial’s value from the dial it is in. The ring is size wide, and the track a quarter of size thick, in the middle of the ring. Its colors, bands, gradient and glow are set by chaining, as with a fill track; bands and a gradient are placed along the whole travel.

A radial fill track may start at the center of the travel, 12 o’clock, instead: it then fills from the center to the value, on either side, as for a pan control.

Example

A dial with a glowing track in a gradient, around a knob 50 across:

#include <elements/element/style/track.hpp>
#include <elements/element/style/dial.hpp>     // For basic_knob
...
using namespace cycfi::elements;
...
auto gain = dial(
   radial_fill_track<20, track_shape::square>(basic_knob<50>())   (1)

      .fill_gradient({
         {0.0f, colors::light_sea_green}
       , {1.0f, colors::medium_orchid}
      }),
   0.8
);

Status Bars

Stock status bars made of a square track and a square fill track. Each takes a value from 0.0 to 1.0, and size, the thickness, 6 unless given; a vertical bar fills from the bottom.

Include

#include <elements/element/style/status_bar.hpp>

Declaration

template <unsigned size = 6, bool vertical = false>
auto
progress_bar(double init_value = 0.0);

template <unsigned size = 6, bool vertical = false>
auto
busy_bar(double init_value = 0.0, double start_pos = 0.0);

template <unsigned size = 6, bool vertical = false>
auto
vu_meter(double init_value = 0.0);

Expressions

Notation

S

Unsigned integer, the thickness of the bar.

V

bool: true for a vertical bar.

v, p

Floating point values from 0.0 to 1.0.

Factory

Expression Semantics

progress_bar<[S, V]>([v])

Make a progress bar at v, 0.0 unless given.

busy_bar<[S, V]>([v, p])

Make a busy bar, its segment from p to v.

vu_meter<[S, V]>([v])

Make a progress bar in green, yellow above 0.7 and red above 0.9.

Each returns the status bar of Status Bars, with its expressions: value(v) sets the value, and a busy bar has start and stop. For other colors, make the bar from tracks:

#include <elements/element/style/status_bar.hpp>
...
using namespace cycfi::elements;
...
auto meter = progress_bar(                                        (1)
   basic_track<6, false, track_shape::square>()
 , basic_fill_track<6, false, track_shape::square>().fill_colors({
      {0.8f, colors::light_sea_green}
    , {1.0f, colors::orange}
   })
);
1 A progress bar in teal, orange above 0.8.
