== Overview

A container holds content and decides how much of it is shown, and where.

* xref:elements/ports.adoc[Ports and Scrollers]: a view of part of an
  element larger than its space, moved by its alignment or by the user.
* xref:elements/child_windows.adoc[Child Windows]: windows drawn inside
  the view, which the user brings to the front, moves, resizes and
  closes.
* xref:elements/splits.adoc[Splits]: tiles whose boundaries the user
  drags.
* xref:elements/docking.adoc[Docking]: child windows the user docks
  along the edges of the view, and floats again.
* xref:elements/popups.adoc[Popups and Modals]: content that opens over
  the view, and modals that block what is under them.
* xref:elements/tooltips.adoc[Tooltips]: tips beside an element, when
  the pointer rests on it.
* xref:elements/menus.adoc[Menus]: menus that buttons open, and the
  shortcuts of their items.
* xref:elements/lists.adoc[Lists]: columns and rows of many items, made
  as they come into view.
