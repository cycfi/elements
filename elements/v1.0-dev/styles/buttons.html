Buttons

Overview

The stock looks of a button: a body with a label and an optional icon, an icon alone, or the frames of a picture. The behavior, momentary, toggle or latching, is on the Buttons page; each look here goes with any of them.

Stock Buttons

button(label), toggle_button(label) and latching_button(label) make a button of each kind with a text label. Each also takes, after the label, a relative size, 1 by default, which scales the text, the icon and the corner radii, and a body_color, the theme’s default_button_color by default:

auto ok = button("OK");
auto mute = toggle_button("Mute", 1.0, colors::red.opacity(0.4));

With an icon (see Icons) as well, the icon goes on the side it is given on: button(icons::cog, "Setup", 1.0) puts it on the left, button("Next", icons::right_circled, 1.0) on the right. The same forms make toggle and latching buttons.

While a button is on, its body is drawn in the active body color, which is the body color unless set, at 70% opacity, moved down and right by a pixel, as if pressed. While the pointer is over it, its text is brighter; while it is disabled, it is dimmed.

The Button Styler

The stock buttons are made with button_styler. Make one with a label, set what it draws with its member functions, and make a button of any kind with it:

auto next = momentary_button(
   button_styler{"Next"}
      .icon(icons::right_circled)
      .align_right()
      .body_color(colors::green.opacity(0.4))
);

Each member function returns a new styler, so the calls chain. If two calls set the same thing, the later one wins: .corner_radius(4) after .rounded_corner_top_left(8) makes all four corners 4. With no calls, a styler draws at size 1, centered, with no icon, in the theme’s colors and button_corner_radius.

A button btn made with a styler is a proxy over it, and the styler holds its label: btn→set_text("Done") changes it, and btn→get_text() reads it.

Icon Buttons

icon_button(code, size, body_color) is a momentary button of an icon on a square body, and toggle_icon_button(code, size, body_color) a toggle. toggle_icon_button(code1, code2, size, body_color) shows code1 while off and code2 while on. plain_icon_button(code, size) has no body: the icon alone.

Sprite Buttons

momentary_button(sp), toggle_button(sp) and latching_button(sp), given a sprite (see Images), make a button that shows one frame of it for each state: frame 0 while off, 1 while off with the pointer over it, 2 while on, and 3 while on with the pointer over it. A sprite with a fifth frame shows it while disabled.

sprite power = sprite{"power_180x632.png", 158 * 0.25, 0.25};
auto power_button = toggle_button(power);

Bodies and Faces

For a button whose content is not a label, button_body(active, body) is the body alone, drawn as a button’s is, free to take any size: put it in a layer under the content. button_face(off, on) is a look made of two elements of any kind, drawn one while the button is off and the other while it is on.

auto play = toggle_button(
   button_face(
      icon{icons::play},
      icon{icons::pause}
   )
);

Expressions

Notation

t

A label, std::string.

i, i2

Icon codes (see Icons).

s

A relative size, float.

c, c2

Colors.

r

A corner radius, float.

tl, tr, br, bl

The radii of the top left, top right, bottom right and bottom left corners, float.

m

A margin, a rect of the space on each side: left, top, right and bottom.

st

A button_styler.

sp

A sprite.

e, e2

Elements.

Semantics

Expression Semantics

button(t, s, c)

A momentary button labelled t, of the relative size s and the body color c.

button(i, t, s, c)

The same, with the icon i on the left of the label.

button(t, i, s, c)

The same, with the icon i on the right of the label.

toggle_button(t, s, c)

A toggle button labelled t, of the relative size s and the body color c. It also takes an icon, as button does.

latching_button(t, s, c)

A latching button labelled t, of the relative size s and the body color c. It also takes an icon, as button does.

button_styler{t}

A styler of the label t.

st.size(s)

A copy of st drawn at the relative size s.

st.body_color(c)

A copy of st with the body color c.

st.active_body_color(c)

A copy of st with the body color c while the button is on.

st.text_color(c)

A copy of st with the label in the color c.

st.icon(i)

A copy of st with the icon i, on the right of the label unless an earlier call placed it.

st.icon_left()

A copy of st with the icon on the left of the label.

st.icon_right()

A copy of st with the icon on the right of the label.

st.icon_color(c)

A copy of st with the icon in the color c.

st.align_left()

A copy of st with the label and icon at the left of the body.

st.align_center()

A copy of st with the label and icon at the center of the body.

st.align_right()

A copy of st with the label and icon at the right of the body.

st.margin(m)

A copy of st with the space m around the label and icon.

st.corner_radius(r)

A copy of st with every corner of radius r.

st.corner_radius(tl, tr, br, bl)

A copy of st with the corners of radius tl, tr, br and bl.

st.rounded_top(r)

A copy of st with the top corners of radius r, and the bottom corners square unless an earlier call set them.

st.rounded_bottom(r)

A copy of st with the bottom corners of radius r, and the top corners square unless an earlier call set them.

st.rounded_left(r)

A copy of st with the left corners of radius r, and the right corners square unless an earlier call set them.

st.rounded_right(r)

A copy of st with the right corners of radius r, and the left corners square unless an earlier call set them.

st.rounded_top()

st.rounded_top(r), with r the theme’s button_corner_radius; the same for rounded_bottom(), rounded_left() and rounded_right().

st.rounded_corner_top_left(r)

A copy of st with the top left corner of radius r, and the others square unless an earlier call set them; the same for rounded_corner_top_right, rounded_corner_bottom_left and rounded_corner_bottom_right.

momentary_button(st)

A momentary button drawn by st.

toggle_button(st)

A toggle button drawn by st.

latching_button(st)

A latching button drawn by st.

icon_button(i, s, c)

A momentary button of the icon i, of the relative size s, on a body of color c.

toggle_icon_button(i, s, c)

A toggle button of the icon i, of the relative size s, on a body of color c.

toggle_icon_button(i, i2, s, c)

A toggle button showing i while off and i2 while on.

plain_icon_button(i, s)

A momentary button of the icon i, of the relative size s, with no body.

momentary_button(sp)

A momentary button showing a frame of sp for each state.

toggle_button(sp)

A toggle button showing a frame of sp for each state.

latching_button(sp)

A latching button showing a frame of sp for each state.

button_body(c, c2)

A button’s body, c while on and c2 while off.

button_face(e, e2)

A look of e while off and e2 while on.
